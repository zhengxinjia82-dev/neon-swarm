Neon Swarm — a neon arena-survival shooter: dodge the swarm, blast geometric enemies, pick upgrades between waves.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 (or open game/index.html directly).

## How to play

- **Move:** WASD or arrow keys. **Aim:** mouse. **Fire:** hold click.
- **Pause:** P or Esc. **Mute:** M. **Restart on game over:** R or click.
- Chasers hunt you, shooters fire back, splitters burst into fast minis, darts are fast.
- Clear a wave and pick 1 of 3 upgrades: fire rate, damage, multishot, speed, hull,
  shield, piercing rounds, or overcharge. Survive as long as you can — your score is
  the goal, and the swarm only gets denser.
